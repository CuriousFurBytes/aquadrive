Typography

Monaspace Argon for UI and Monaspace Krypton for code. All sizes scale from a 16px root.

Headings

Normal text & subtitle

Subtitles sit directly beneath headings to give them context.

Body copy uses Monaspace Argon at 16px with a 1.55 line height. Paragraphs are separated by a full line of space so dense prose stays readable without forcing justified text.

Links inherit their color and are underlined using the active accent: an in-line example. Hovered links thicken the underline and set the text to bold.

Inline styles

Bold text for emphasis, accent-bold for product terms, italic text for titles, strikethrough to show removal and underlined text for callouts.
