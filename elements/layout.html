What products do you use?
We exclusively use pH-neutral, biodegradable car care products. Our ceramic coatings are professional-grade SiO2 formulations that provide long-lasting protection without harming the environment.
How long does a detail take?
A basic exterior wash takes about 30 minutes. A full interior and exterior detail takes 3-4 hours. Ceramic coating applications require 4-6 hours plus a 24-hour cure time.
Do you come to my location?
Yes, our mobile detailing team comes to your home or office. We bring all equipment, water, and power. We just need access to your vehicle and a shaded area is preferred.
What is your cancellation policy?
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a $25 fee.
